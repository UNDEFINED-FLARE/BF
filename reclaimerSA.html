South African Student Revolution

#ReclaimSA is a youth-driven and citizen-powered movement dedicated to effective and positive change in South Africa. Through community action, accountability monitoring, and the strategic mobilisation of its members’ skills, #ReclaimSA works towards a future defined by ethical leadership, economic justice, and united communities. The movement is independent and funded by the contributions of its members.
We are reclaiming SA! Our power does not lie in a ballot box alone. It lies in our hands, our skills, and our unwavering commitment to each other. #ReclaimSA is a citizen-powered platform turning frustration into focused action. We channel our collective energy into tangible projects that rebuild our communities and hold power to account.
